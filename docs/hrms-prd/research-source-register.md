## How to use this register

- Any claim in [01-market-research.md](01-market-research.md) or [02-competitor-comparison-matrix.md](02-competitor-comparison-matrix.md) marked 🟡/🟠/🔴, or explicitly flagged UNVERIFIED/NOT FOUND, should be **independently re-confirmed** (direct page re-fetch, trial signup, or sales-team contact) before being used in any customer-facing competitive-positioning material, investor material, or legal/compliance claim.
- Several vendor sites (g2.com, keka.com, rippling.com) blocked automated fetching during this research pass (HTTP 403 or TLS errors) — this is a tooling limitation of this research pass, not evidence about the vendors themselves, and should not be over-interpreted.
- This register should be refreshed alongside [01-market-research.md](01-market-research.md) and [02-competitor-comparison-matrix.md](02-competitor-comparison-matrix.md) — recommended cadence: every 2 quarters, or before any major competitive-positioning decision.
