## Open questions

- OQ-16: This matrix reflects point-in-time research (2026-07-23) against fast-moving competitor products (several cells reflect features shipped as recently as Nov 2025). Recommend refreshing this matrix no less than every 2 quarters during active PRD/roadmap use.
- OQ-17: Cells marked ❓ are genuine research gaps, not confirmed absences — before using any ❓ cell to justify a "competitor lacks X" claim in positioning material, do a direct, current check (trial signup, sales demo) rather than relying on this document alone.
