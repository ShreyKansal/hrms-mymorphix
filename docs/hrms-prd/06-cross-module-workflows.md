# 06 — Cross-Module Workflows

**Status:** Draft v1 (pending stakeholder review)
**Last updated:** 2026-07-23
**Purpose:** the 25 end-to-end workflows the brief requires, each spanning multiple modules. Module-level detail already documented in the relevant module PRD is referenced, not repeated — this document's job is the *seams* between modules, which is where competitor research (market research §8) found the most consistent failure pattern (one-way sync, disconnected handoffs, manual reconciliation).

---

## 1. Candidate hired and converted into employee

**Trigger:** Offer accepted (Module 8 §7.2). **Preconditions:** Offer status = Accepted, joining date set. **Actor:** System (automatic), Recruiter/HR Executive (review). **Steps:** Module 8 fires `CandidateHired` → Module 1 creates a Draft Employee record pre-populated from the candidate profile (zero re-entry, per Module 8 §7.3) → Module 3 onboarding checklist instantiated automatically → Preboarding Portal access provisioned. **Decision points:** Onboarding-template selection (role/department/location-driven). **Approval logic:** None additional — the offer approval (Module 8 §7.2) was the authorising decision. **System actions:** Bidirectional link retained between the Module 8 candidate record and the new Module 1 employee record, so recruitment-funnel analytics remain accurate after conversion. **Notifications:** New hire (portal invite), HR, hiring manager. **Failure handling:** Missing mandatory candidate data blocks Draft-record creation with a specific, actionable gap list, not a silent partial record. **Final outcome:** Draft Employee record + active onboarding checklist, fully traceable to source candidate. **Audit events:** `CandidateHired`, `EmployeeCreated`, `OnboardingInitiated`, cross-referenced.

## 2. Employee onboarding

**Trigger:** Draft Employee record created (Workflow #1) or direct entry (Module 1 §7.1). **Preconditions:** Checklist templates configured (Module 22). **Actor:** New hire, HR, IT, Finance, Manager. **Steps:** Full detail in Module 3 §7.1–§7.3. **Decision points:** Minimum checklist-completion threshold for Draft→Active activation (Module 3 §10). **Approval logic:** Document verification is a lightweight per-item approval (Module 3 §13). **System actions:** Asset allocation trigger (Module 14 §7.1), IT account provisioning trigger (Module 23), mandatory-training enrolment trigger (Module 10 §7.1), policy-acknowledgement trigger (Module 20 §7.1). **Notifications:** All checklist stakeholders per Module 3 §19. **Failure handling:** No-show/deferred-joining paths (Module 3 §7.3). **Final outcome:** Employee record transitions Draft → Active (Module 1 §14). **Audit events:** Per Module 3 §22.

## 3. Employee transfer between departments

**Trigger:** HR/Manager initiates a transfer (Module 1 §7.2). **Preconditions:** Actor has scope over both source and target departments. **Actor:** HR Executive/Administrator. **Steps:** New Employment Assignment record created with `reason_code = "Transfer"`, effective date, per [05-organisation-data-model.md](05-organisation-data-model.md) §7. **Decision points:** Payroll-group change if departments span different groups (Module 1 §8 table). **Approval logic:** Losing + gaining department heads (Module 1 §13). **System actions:** Manager, HRBP, Cost Centre reassignment; in-flight-approval re-routing rule applied if manager changes concurrently (Module 17 §7.1's inactive-approver handling, analogously). **Notifications:** Old/new manager, HRBP, employee, Payroll if payroll-relevant. **Failure handling:** Retroactive-dated transfers into a locked payroll period redirect to the correction path (Module 1 §7.3), never the standard path. **Final outcome:** New effective-dated Employment Assignment; org chart reflects it from the effective date forward. **Audit events:** `EmployeeTransferred`.

## 4. Employee transfer between legal entities

**Trigger:** Same as #3, but Legal Entity changes. **Preconditions:** Same, plus the continuity-of-service question flagged as **OQ-9 (blocking, needs qualified legal/payroll input)** in [05-organisation-data-model.md](05-organisation-data-model.md) §8. **Actor:** HR Administrator + Payroll Administrator (mandatory dual involvement given settlement implications, Module 1 §13). **Steps:** Depending on the OQ-9 resolution, either (a) continuous-service treatment — a straightforward Employment Assignment change, or (b) exit-and-rejoin treatment — triggers a partial Module 15 settlement in the old entity and a fresh Module 3 onboarding in the new one. **Decision points:** This branch point is the crux of the whole workflow and **cannot be resolved by this PRD alone**. **Approval logic:** HR Administrator + Payroll Administrator co-approval regardless of branch. **System actions:** Payroll Group, statutory registrations, cost-centre reassignment. **Notifications:** Both entities' HR/Payroll teams. **Failure handling:** N/A until OQ-9 resolves. **Final outcome:** New Legal Entity assignment, with settlement/rejoin artifacts if branch (b). **Audit events:** `EmployeeTransferred` (type = Inter-Entity), plus settlement/rejoin events if applicable.

## 5. Employee promotion

**Trigger:** Manager/HR initiates, or Module 9 §7.1's promotion-recommendation output. **Preconditions:** Grade/Band change proposed (Module 1 §6). **Actor:** Manager (recommends), HR/relevant approver (approves per Module 1 §13's grade/band threshold). **Steps:** New Employment Assignment (`reason_code = "Promotion"`) with new Grade/Designation/Band, typically paired with a Compensation change (Module 1 §8). **Decision points:** Approval threshold by Band (per [05-organisation-data-model.md](05-organisation-data-model.md) §6). **Approval logic:** Module 17, threshold-configured. **System actions:** Module 6 salary-revision processing trigger, Module 13 promotion-letter generation trigger (Module 13 §7.1). **Notifications:** Employee, manager, Payroll. **Failure handling:** Rejected recommendation returns to Draft with reason. **Final outcome:** New Employment Assignment + Compensation record + generated letter. **Audit events:** `EmployeePromoted`.

## 6. Salary revision

**Trigger:** Annual cycle, off-cycle request, or promotion-linked (#5). **Preconditions:** Grade/Band-consistent proposed compensation (Module 1 §11). **Actor:** Manager (recommends via Module 16), HR/Payroll Administrator (approves). **Steps:** New effective-dated Compensation record (Module 1 §8, distinct entity from Employment Assignment). **Decision points:** Amount/percentage threshold routing (Module 1 §13). **Approval logic:** Module 17. **System actions:** Module 6 §7.1 payroll-input update from the revision's effective date forward; if effective date is retroactive into a locked period, routes through Module 6 §7.3's arrears mechanism instead. **Notifications:** Employee (compensation-revision notice, Module 6 §19), Payroll. **Failure handling:** Rejected revision returns to submitter. **Final outcome:** New Compensation record, payroll reflects it from the correct cycle. **Audit events:** `CompensationRevised`, `RetroactiveSalaryRevisionProcessed` if applicable.

## 7. Manager change

**Trigger:** Standalone reassignment, or a side effect of #3. **Preconditions:** New manager is Active and not creating a circular reporting reference ([05-organisation-data-model.md](05-organisation-data-model.md) §4). **Actor:** HR Executive/Administrator. **Steps:** New Employment Assignment's Functional Manager field updated (Module 1 §8). **Decision points:** In-flight approvals awaiting the old manager — explicit policy needed (Module 1 §8's flagged open question): auto-reassign to new manager, or let old manager finish pending approvals. **Approval logic:** Typically HR-only, no cross-department approval needed unless paired with #3. **System actions:** Module 17 re-routes pending workflow instances per the resolved policy. **Notifications:** Old/new manager, employee, HRBP. **Failure handling:** N/A beyond standard validation. **Final outcome:** New Employment Assignment; org chart updates from effective date. **Audit events:** `EmployeeTransferred` (or a dedicated manager-change event, implementation detail).

## 8. Location change

**Trigger:** Employee/HR-initiated relocation or WFH-arrangement change. **Preconditions:** New Branch/Work Location exists (Module 2). **Actor:** HR Executive. **Steps:** New Employment Assignment with updated Branch/Work Location, potentially triggering Holiday Calendar and Attendance Policy Group reassignment (Module 1 §8 table). **Decision points:** PT (Professional Tax) implication if Branch changes state (Module 6 compliance impact). **Approval logic:** Typically HR-only, Payroll-notified if PT-relevant. **System actions:** Module 4 geo-fencing rule update, Module 6 statutory-configuration check. **Notifications:** Employee, Payroll (if PT-relevant). **Failure handling:** N/A. **Final outcome:** New Employment Assignment; attendance/payroll systems reflect the new location from effective date. **Audit events:** `EmployeeTransferred` (type = Location Change).

## 9. Shift change

**Trigger:** Business need, employee request, or a location change (#8). **Preconditions:** Target Shift Group exists (Module 4 §9). **Actor:** Manager/HR. **Steps:** Shift Group reassignment (effective-dated per Module 1 §7's pattern, applied to this attribute). **Decision points:** Overtime-eligibility recalculation if the new shift changes eligibility rules (Module 4 §10). **Approval logic:** Manager-level, low-risk. **System actions:** Module 4 attendance-policy recalculation from effective date. **Notifications:** Employee, manager. **Failure handling:** N/A. **Final outcome:** New Shift Group in effect. **Audit events:** Shift-reassignment event (Module 4 audit trail).

## 10. Leave affecting payroll

**Trigger:** Leave approved (Module 5 §7.1) of an unpaid/LOP-relevant type, or year-end encashment (Module 5 §7.3). **Preconditions:** Leave finalised, not in a still-open regularisation-eligible window. **Actor:** System (automatic handoff). **Steps:** Module 5 hands off LOP-days/encashment-amount as a Module 6 §7.1 payroll input. **Decision points:** None — this is a deterministic data handoff by the time it reaches Payroll, per Module 4 §25's "no ambiguity at handoff" acceptance criterion (Leave mirrors this same discipline). **Approval logic:** Already resolved upstream in Module 5's own approval chain. **System actions:** Module 6 computes LOP deduction or encashment payment. **Notifications:** Employee (via payslip, Module 6 §19). **Failure handling:** Unresolved leave status at payroll-input-collection time blocks that employee's inclusion in the run until resolved (Module 6 §11), never a silent guess. **Final outcome:** Payslip reflects correct LOP/encashment. **Audit events:** `PayrollInputChanged` (source = Leave).

## 11. Attendance regularisation affecting payroll

**Trigger:** Regularisation approved (Module 4 §7.2) before the attendance-lock cutoff (Module 4 §7.3). **Preconditions:** Within the regularisation window. **Actor:** System (automatic handoff, same pattern as #10). **Steps:** Regularised attendance value feeds Module 6 §7.1 as LOP/present-days input. **Decision points:** If regularisation is requested *after* lock, it must route through Module 4 §7.3's post-lock dual-sign-off correction path, which feeds Module 6 §7.3's arrears mechanism instead of the standard run. **Approval logic:** Manager (regularisation) + HR Administrator/Payroll Executive (post-lock correction only). **System actions:** Module 6 recalculates the affected input. **Notifications:** Employee, Payroll. **Failure handling:** Same as #10. **Final outcome:** Correct payroll input, on-cycle or via arrears. **Audit events:** `AttendanceRegularised`, `PayrollInputChanged`.

## 12. Reimbursement paid through payroll

**Trigger:** Expense claim fully approved (Module 7 §7.1) with payroll-routed payout configured. **Preconditions:** Claim approved, not flagged as duplicate. **Actor:** System (automatic handoff). **Steps:** Approved claim queued as a Module 6 §7.1 one-time-payment input for the next cycle. **Decision points:** Taxability per expense category (Module 6 §9's statutory-treatment-per-component rule). **Approval logic:** Already resolved in Module 7's own chain. **System actions:** Module 6 applies the correct tax treatment automatically per the category's configured rule. **Notifications:** Employee (payslip line item). **Failure handling:** A component with undefined statutory treatment blocks inclusion (Module 6 §11) — no ad hoc payroll line items. **Final outcome:** Reimbursement appears correctly on the payslip. **Audit events:** `ReimbursementPaid`, `PayrollInputChanged`.

## 13. Performance review leading to promotion

**Trigger:** Review cycle closes with a promotion recommendation (Module 9 §9). **Preconditions:** Calibration complete (Module 9 §7.2). **Actor:** Manager (recommends), HR/Department Head (approves). **Steps:** Feeds directly into Workflow #5 — the recommendation does not itself execute a promotion, it initiates Module 1's standard approval-gated process (Module 9 §10's explicit no-bypass rule). **Decision points:** Same as #5. **Approval logic:** Module 1 §13's grade/band threshold, not a separate performance-specific approval track. **System actions:** Module 13 promotion letter, Module 6 compensation processing. **Notifications:** Employee, manager. **Failure handling:** Recommendation not acted on within a reasonable window should be visible in HR reporting (a process-health signal, Module 9 §21), not silently forgotten. **Final outcome:** Same as Workflow #5's final outcome. **Audit events:** Links Module 9's recommendation event to Module 1's `EmployeePromoted` event.

## 14. Probation completion and confirmation

**Trigger:** Probation end date approaching/reached (Module 1 employment data). **Preconditions:** No open disciplinary/performance-improvement process blocking confirmation. **Actor:** Manager (confirms or extends/escalates), HR (processes). **Steps:** Manager reviews probation performance, confirms via Module 16's Manager Home (Module 16 §9), or requests extension/initiates a PIP (Module 9 §9) if performance is a concern. **Decision points:** Confirm / Extend / Do-not-confirm (leading toward Workflow #16's termination path). **Approval logic:** Manager decision, HR Administrator sign-off for non-confirmation given its separation-adjacent sensitivity (Module 1 §13's low-frequency-high-risk classification). **System actions:** Module 1 employment-status update; Module 13 confirmation-letter generation (Module 13 §7.1). **Notifications:** Employee, manager, HR. **Failure handling:** No action taken by the probation end date should escalate (not silently lapse into an ambiguous status) — Module 1's status model should not allow indefinite "still on probation past the end date" drift. **Final outcome:** Confirmed status, or an extension/PIP/non-confirmation path initiated. **Audit events:** `EmployeeUpdated` (status = Confirmed), or linkage to Module 9/15 events for the alternative paths.

## 15. Resignation and final settlement

**Trigger:** Employee resignation (Module 15 §7.1). **Preconditions:** Employee is Active. **Actor:** Employee, Manager, HR, Payroll Administrator. **Steps:** Full detail in Module 15 §7.1/§7.4 and Module 6 §7.4 — notice-period reconciliation → exit checklist (spanning Modules 4, 5, 6, 13, 14) → asset recovery (Module 14 §7.2) → access revocation (Module 15 §7.3) → final settlement calculation (leave encashment, gratuity, loan recovery, less recoverable dues) → Payroll Administrator approval → relieving/experience letters (Module 13). **Decision points:** Notice-period waiver/recovery (Module 15 §7.1); outstanding-asset-vs-settlement-release policy (Module 14 §7.2). **Approval logic:** HR Administrator (notice-period decisions), Payroll Administrator (final settlement, Module 6 §13's two-role pattern). **System actions:** Module 1 status → Separated (terminal); alumni-status transition. **Notifications:** All exit-checklist stakeholders. **Failure handling:** Incomplete mandatory checklist items block terminal-status transition (Module 15 §10). **Final outcome:** Employee separated, settled, alumni status active, rehire-eligibility flagged. **Audit events:** `FullAndFinalSettlementProcessed`, `SeparationInitiated`→ terminal.

## 16. Termination

**Trigger:** Manager/HR-initiated for cause, performance (post-PIP), or redundancy (Module 15 §7.2). **Preconditions:** HR Administrator involvement from initiation (not just exceptions, unlike resignation). **Actor:** HR Administrator (mandatory), Manager (initiates/documents cause). **Steps:** Same exit-checklist/settlement mechanics as #15, but with mandatory documented justification and (flagged, not asserted) likely jurisdiction-dependent legal review before finalisation (Module 15 §28, blocking open question). **Decision points:** Immediate-effect vs. notice-period-served termination — affects Module 15 §7.3's access-revocation timing (immediate vs. end-of-notice). **Approval logic:** HR Administrator, with legal/compliance co-sign where the tenant's policy requires it. **System actions:** Same as #15, plus immediate access-revocation branch if applicable. **Notifications:** Restricted distribution given sensitivity (HR/Payroll/IT only, not broadcast). **Failure handling:** Same as #15. **Final outcome:** Same as #15, tagged distinctly (type = Termination) for legal-defensibility and reporting purposes. **Audit events:** `SeparationInitiated` (type = Termination), full documented-justification trail.

## 17. Employee rehire

**Trigger:** A former employee (alumni status, Module 15 §9) is being hired again, either via Workflow #1's normal recruitment path or a direct offer. **Preconditions:** Rehire-eligibility flag is not blocking (Module 15 §9). **Actor:** Recruiter/HR. **Steps:** At candidate-application or offer stage (Module 8), the system should detect the match against the former-employee record (Module 8 §24's edge case) and surface the historical employment record to the recruiter/HR rather than treating them as unknown. On conversion (Workflow #1), the new Employee record should **relink to historical data** (prior Employment Assignment history, prior documents) rather than creating a fully disconnected duplicate (Module 1 §24's rehire edge case). **Decision points:** Whether prior tenure counts toward any tenure-dependent benefit (gratuity eligibility, leave accrual tier) — a genuine policy question, **flagged for legal/HR-policy input**, not assumed either way. **Approval logic:** Standard Module 8 offer approval, plus HR Administrator awareness given the rehire context. **System actions:** Historical-record relinking. **Notifications:** HR (rehire flag surfaced). **Failure handling:** If historical data was purged per a data-retention policy (Module 20 §10) before the rehire, the system should clearly indicate "prior record unavailable" rather than silently starting fresh without flagging the gap. **Final outcome:** New employment period linked to (not replacing) historical data. **Audit events:** `CandidateHired` with a rehire flag, `EmployeeCreated` with a `previous_employee_reference`.

## 18. Employee data correction after payroll closure

**Trigger:** An error is discovered in Module 1 master data or Module 4 attendance data for an already-locked/paid period. **Preconditions:** Requires elevated permission. **Actor:** HR Administrator (initiates), Payroll Administrator (co-approves). **Steps:** Full detail in Module 1 §7.3 and Module 4 §7.3's post-lock correction paths — a new, effective-dated record supersedes but never deletes the erroneous one; dual sign-off required; feeds Module 6 §7.3's arrears/off-cycle-adjustment mechanism for any payroll-relevant impact. **Decision points:** Whether the correction is payroll-relevant at all (some Module 1 corrections, e.g., a typo in an address, have no payroll impact and don't need Payroll Administrator involvement — only compensation/statutory/attendance-relevant corrections do). **Approval logic:** Dual sign-off for payroll-relevant corrections specifically. **System actions:** Module 6 arrears calculation if applicable. **Notifications:** Employee (if the correction affects their pay), Payroll. **Failure handling:** Payroll Administrator rejection returns the correction request with reason. **Final outcome:** Corrected historical record, fully traceable, with the original erroneous record preserved (not erased) for audit. **Audit events:** Full before/after, both approvers, explicit "retroactively corrected" flag (Module 1 §7.3).

## 19. Retroactive salary revision

**Trigger:** A compensation change (Workflow #6) is approved with an effective date in an already-processed payroll period. **Preconditions:** Same as #18. **Actor:** Payroll Executive (calculates arrears), Payroll Administrator (approves). **Steps:** Full detail in Module 6 §7.3 — arrears computed as the difference between paid and correct amounts for each affected period, statutory recalculation applied where relevant (**flagged for qualified payroll-professional review**, per Module 6 §28), arrears queued as a one-time payment in the next/an off-cycle run. **Decision points:** Include in next regular run vs. a dedicated off-cycle run — tenant preference. **Approval logic:** Standard Module 6 §13 review process, same rigor as any payroll input. **System actions:** Statutory-contribution recalculation (PF impact of a backdated basic-salary change, etc.). **Notifications:** Employee (arrears payment explanation, not just a mysterious extra line item on the payslip). **Failure handling:** Same variance-review gate as any payroll input (Module 6 §7.1). **Final outcome:** Arrears paid, statutory records correctly reflect the retroactive change. **Audit events:** `RetroactiveSalaryRevisionProcessed`.

## 20. Role and permission change

**Trigger:** A user's role/scope needs to change (job change, temporary grant, revocation). **Preconditions:** Actor has role-administration permission (Module 21 §12). **Actor:** System/HR Administrator. **Steps:** Full detail in Module 21 §7.1 — role/scope assignment, segregation-of-duties conflict check, automatic expiry for time-boxed grants. **Decision points:** Segregation-of-duties conflict detected → block-by-default with logged override path (Module 21 §10). **Approval logic:** Logged justification minimum; second-approver for highest-risk overrides (tenant-configurable). **System actions:** Immediate effect for permission *reductions* (security-first); per Module 21 §24's explicit design decision. **Notifications:** Affected user, granting administrator, compliance contact (for overrides). **Failure handling:** N/A beyond the conflict-check gate. **Final outcome:** Updated effective permission set, immediately enforced at the backend regardless of UI state (Module 21 §25's core acceptance criterion). **Audit events:** `RoleAssigned`, `PermissionScopeChanged`.

## 21. Organisation restructuring

**Trigger:** Leadership-approved reorg (Module 2 §7.1). **Preconditions:** Affected employee list identified and reviewed. **Actor:** HR Administrator. **Steps:** Full detail in Module 2 §7.1 — org-unit entity changes + batch Employment Assignment reassignment sharing a common `reorg_event` reference and `reason_code`, preview-before-commit, atomic all-or-nothing commit. **Decision points:** Any employee left without a valid manager/department post-reorg blocks commit until resolved. **Approval logic:** Leadership/Finance sign-off captured outside the system or via Module 17, per tenant configuration. **System actions:** Bulk Employment Assignment creation (Module 1 §7.2 mechanics, batched); org-unit deactivation. **Notifications:** Single consolidated notification per affected employee, not fragmented individual-field-change notices. **Failure handling:** Partial-commit explicitly disallowed. **Final outcome:** New org structure in effect from the specified date; historical structure remains queryable for prior dates (Module 2 §25). **Audit events:** `OrganisationRestructured`, linked to the batch's individual `EmployeeTransferred` events.

## 22. Bulk employee import

**Trigger:** New tenant onboarding (Module 22 §7.1) or an ongoing bulk-correction need. **Preconditions:** Import template correctly formatted. **Actor:** HR Administrator/System Administrator. **Steps:** Column-mapping UI → pre-import validation report (never silent partial failure, Module 1 §18) → duplicate-detection against government IDs (Module 1 §11) → dry-run/preview → commit. **Decision points:** Any row failing validation is excluded from commit with a specific, exportable error list — the import either proceeds with the valid subset (clearly reported) or is fully rejected, per a tenant-configurable policy, never a silent partial-and-unreported result. **Approval logic:** HR Administrator-tier action given the blast radius (Module 1 §12). **System actions:** Bulk Employee/Employment Assignment record creation. **Notifications:** Import-completion summary (success/error counts) to the initiating administrator. **Failure handling:** Full validation-error report, re-importable after correction. **Final outcome:** New employee records created, fully consistent with the standard data model (no special "imported" record type that behaves differently later). **Audit events:** `BulkImportCompleted`, with the full error/success manifest retained.

## 23. Payroll rollback

**Trigger:** Material error discovered after Process-and-Lock, before or immediately after disbursal (Module 6 §7.2). **Preconditions:** Payroll Administrator-tier action, mandatory documented reason, recommended second-approver. **Actor:** Payroll Administrator. **Steps:** Full detail in Module 6 §7.2 — system checks bank-transfer-execution status first; if not yet executed, run reverts to Pending, payslips withdrawn with an employee-facing notice, corrected run re-processes; if already executed, this is explicitly **not** a clean rollback but a financial-recovery process (Module 6 §7.2's honesty-in-UI requirement). **Decision points:** Funds-moved-or-not is the critical branch. **Approval logic:** Dual sign-off strongly recommended given blast radius. **System actions:** Payslip withdrawal notice (Module 18), audit-trail preservation of the original run alongside the rollback. **Notifications:** Employees (payslip-withdrawn notice), Payroll Administrator (confirmation). **Failure handling:** N/A — this workflow *is* the failure-handling path for a prior run. **Final outcome:** Either a clean pre-disbursal correction, or a flagged financial-recovery process outside normal system rollback semantics. **Audit events:** `PayrollRunRolledBack`, with bank-transfer-status-at-time-of-rollback explicitly recorded.

## 24. Failed integration recovery

**Trigger:** Any Module 23 integration failure (Module 23 §7.1). **Preconditions:** N/A. **Actor:** IT Administrator (typically), or the relevant module's persona for business-context awareness (e.g., Payroll Administrator for a banking-integration failure, Module 15's IT Administrator for an access-revocation-integration failure). **Steps:** Failure detected and logged → severity-differentiated alert fires (Module 23 §7.1's differentiated-urgency principle — a job-board posting failure is not a payroll-disbursal failure) → manual-resync capability always available (never a dead end requiring vendor-support intervention) → root-cause investigation and, where the integration partner's API/schema changed, field-mapping reconfiguration (Module 23 §7.1). **Decision points:** Whether the failure blocks a dependent workflow (e.g., Module 15's access revocation) pending manual intervention, or whether the dependent workflow has its own fallback (e.g., manual access revocation by IT while the automated integration is fixed). **Approval logic:** N/A, an operational-recovery workflow, not an approval-gated one. **System actions:** Retry per the integration's defined idempotency-safe retry strategy (Module 23 §7.1 — never a naive retry on a payment-type integration). **Notifications:** Per severity, per Module 18's critical-override rules. **Failure handling:** This *is* the failure-handling workflow. **Final outcome:** Integration restored, or a documented manual-fallback process activated until it is. **Audit events:** `IntegrationSyncFailed`, `IntegrationManualResyncTriggered`.

## 25. Data deletion or anonymisation request

**Trigger:** A legally-valid data-subject deletion/anonymisation request (e.g., under applicable Indian data-protection law) for a former employee, candidate, or (rarely) current employee. **Preconditions:** Request verified as legitimate and legally actionable (a legal/compliance determination outside this PRD's scope to adjudicate, but the system must support acting on the determination once made). **Actor:** HR Administrator/Compliance-Audit User (processes), with System Administrator involvement for the underlying data operation. **Steps:** 1) Request logged and routed for legal/compliance review 2) System checks for an active legal hold (Module 20 §10) on the affected records — **a legal hold takes hard precedence and blocks deletion/anonymisation until lifted**, regardless of the deletion request's own validity, since the two obligations can genuinely conflict and the hold must win 3) If no legal hold blocks it, the system performs the deletion/anonymisation per a defined technical process (which fields are hard-deleted vs. anonymised vs. retained under a separate statutory-retention obligation — e.g., certain payroll/statutory records may have their own mandatory retention period that also survives an individual deletion request, a genuine tension **flagged for legal review**, not resolved by this PRD) 4) Confirmation and an audit record of the action (not the deleted content itself, obviously, but the fact and scope of the action) are retained. **Decision points:** Legal-hold conflict (§step 2); statutory-retention-vs-deletion-request conflict (§step 3). **Approval logic:** Legal/Compliance sign-off required before execution, given the irreversibility and legal sensitivity. **System actions:** Cross-module deletion/anonymisation execution (this necessarily touches Module 1, potentially Module 6/8/13's records for that individual). **Notifications:** Requesting individual (confirmation once complete, per legal requirement), internal compliance record. **Failure handling:** A legal hold blocks execution entirely until lifted — the request is held, not silently dropped or silently executed in violation of the hold. **Final outcome:** Records deleted/anonymised per the resolved scope, with an audit record of the action itself surviving (a deliberate, necessary exception to "deletion means deletion" — the fact that a deletion occurred, and its legal basis, must remain provable). **Audit events:** `DataDeletionRequested`, `DataDeletionExecuted` (or `DataDeletionBlockedByLegalHold`).

## Open questions

- OQ-21: Workflow #4 (inter-entity transfer) is genuinely blocked on OQ-9's legal resolution — this is the single most consequential open question carried through this document, since it determines whether a whole category of transfers is a simple data change or a settlement-and-rejoin event.
- OQ-22: Workflow #17 (rehire)'s tenure-continuity question (does prior service count toward gratuity/leave-tier eligibility on rehire) needs explicit legal/HR-policy input, not an assumed default either way.
- OQ-23: Workflow #25's exact technical boundary between "must delete," "must anonymise," and "must retain despite the request" (per conflicting statutory-retention obligations) needs dedicated legal review before this workflow's detailed technical design proceeds.
