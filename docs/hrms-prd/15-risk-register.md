# 15 — Risk Register

**Status:** Draft v1 (pending stakeholder review)
**Last updated:** 2026-07-23
**Depends on:** every module's own §27 Risks section (this document consolidates the highest-severity ones and adds product/technical risks that span modules)

**Risk-score convention:** Probability (Low/Medium/High) × Impact (Low/Medium/High/Severe) → informal score, not a numeric formula — this phase prioritises directional judgment over false-precision scoring.
